# Wild Frontier — Game Brief

**Genre:** Open-world western RPG, top-down 3/4 pixel art
**Platform:** Web browser (desktop; keyboard and mouse, gamepad partial)
**Setting:** Calder Territory, a fictional western territory, April 1888
**Pillars**
1. **The horse is a character.** Every horse is a simulated individual you bond with through care and shared danger.
2. **A living frontier.** Towns run on schedules, wildlife lives by ecology, weather and night change how you play.
3. **Grounded, morally grey story.** It's a mystery about water, land and a forged survey, not a revenge plot.
4. **Curiosity is rewarded.** Hidden places, documents, legendary horses and side stories are off the main path.

## Premise
You're a horse-breaker and former army remount wrangler. Your uncle Silas Brannigan sends for you
to help with the spring drive. When you arrive, his horses have been rustled and Silas is missing.
The trail leads to:
- a railroad land agent who forged the territorial survey to take the Calder River water rights,
- the outlaw gang he pays to break the ranchers,
- the surveyor who drew the honest line,
- and the Sayen Nation, whose treaty boundary the true route follows.

You decide what to do with the evidence: the marshal, the press, or the money.

## Core loop
Ride → explore and discover → work (quests, hunting, trading, racing, bounties) → care for your horse
and yourself (feed, water, groom, treat, camp, sleep) → earn money, reputation and bond → better horses,
gear and access → farther and harder country.

## Regions
- **Dustfall Plains:** central grassland, horse and cattle country. Contains Larkspur and the Brannigan Ranch.
- **Blackwater Basin:** lake, money and rail. Contains Port Ashby.
- **Pine Hollow:** forest, logging and Sayen Valley.
- **High Sierra:** snow, passes, Whitepeak and the trapper's cabin.
- **The Drylands:** desert, silver mines, Sulfur Flats and the Mission ruins.
- **Redstone Canyon:** mesas, Fort Rourke and Coyle's Roost.
