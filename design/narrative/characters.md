# Wild Frontier — Characters

> Canon level: **Established** for every NPC in `src/data/story/npcs.ts` and
> the ids below. Secrets are "true answers" even if the player never learns
> them. Dialogue must match the voice notes.

Format: **Name** (`id`) — settlement / workplace — role.
*Motivation* · *Secret* · *Voice*.

## The Protagonist
Player-named and gender-neutral. A former Army remount wrangler and Silas's
kin. Journals are a first-person diary: dry, observant, never naming
themselves. NPCs call them "Brannigan's kin", "partner", "friend" or
"stranger".

## Story Cast

**Silas Brannigan** (`silas` at Hollow Crown; `silas_ranch` at the ranch house once `silas_home` is set). Owner of the Rocking B.
*Motivation:* keep the ranch and the valley's old water-sharing ways. *Secret:* he followed the horses alone partly from pride, and he knows it. He has proposed to Minerva five times. *Voice:* gruff and self-mocking, with short sentences and ranch metaphors. He can't say thank you without a joke.

**Minerva Lindqvist** (`minerva`) — Hollow Crown — trapper.
*Motivation:* to be left alone, mostly, and to keep Silas alive out of spite. *Secret:* she'd say yes if she thought he'd stop asking. *Voice:* sardonic, practical and Swedish-precise. She gives cold-weather advice as if it were insults.

**Hollis Tolliver** (`hollis`) — Brannigan ranch — foreman, wounded.
*Motivation:* hold the ranch until Silas returns. *Secret:* he's ashamed he obeyed Silas and stayed behind. *Voice:* Tejano cowman, blunt, with a porch philosopher's timing. He uses "ain't" and avoids sentiment.

**Josiah Oakes** (`josiah`) — Larkspur livery — liveryman, Cup steward.
*Motivation:* keep Larkspur decent. *Secret:* he loaned Silas $200 last winter and has told no one. *Voice:* warm, wry Kentucky cadence, gently teasing. He has opinions about Biscuit.

**Ellery Vance** (`vance`) — Port Ashby depot — M&W land agent.
*Motivation:* the valley will be sold anyway, so why not to him. *Secret:* he owns the Calder Valley Townsite & Water Co. through straw men, forged the survey, and pays Coyle. *Voice:* smooth, courteous and precise, with threats phrased as "observations". He never raises his voice.

**Dutch Coyle** (`dutch_coyle`) — appears at the ranch during the showdown only. Outlaw boss.
*Motivation:* respectability: an Army contract and a ranch with a porch. *Secret:* born Dietrich Kohl. He robbed Hester Ketterman in 1884. *Voice:* well-read, wry and polite, enjoys a good phrase, insists on "civilised". He never draws early.

**Tobias Finch** (`rustler_finch`) — Coyle's Roost — teen rustler.
*Motivation:* survive. *Secret:* he unloaded his gun before the player arrived because he couldn't face using it. *Voice:* breathless and ungrammatical ("I ain't", "he come"), scared and honest.

**Thaddeus Pruitt** (`pruitt`) — Mission San Isidro — Deputy Surveyor.
*Motivation:* the truth of the line, and food. *Secret:* he wrote "Gone to the saints" hoping C. (Cordelia) would find it. *Voice:* fussy, precise and nervously funny, with surveying jargon ("bearing", "chainage", "station"). He is brave only in theory.

**Cordelia Fontaine** (`cordelia`) — Port Ashby, Clarion office — editor.
*Motivation:* the truth, printed right and first. *Secret:* the Clarion is two months from bankruptcy, and the story would save it. She chooses the Sayen statement over speed anyway. *Voice:* quick, cutting and witty. She talks in headlines and deadlines.

**Marshal Ruth Kincaid** (`kincaid`) — Port Ashby — Territorial Marshal.
*Motivation:* cases that hold. *Secret:* the governor has twice asked her to resign, and she has kept both letters. *Voice:* clipped and procedural, with a dry kindness underneath: "Witnesses keep men honest. Including me."

**Lt. Miles Abernathy** (`abernathy`) — Fort Rourke — quartermaster.
*Motivation:* do his duty, and then undo his mistake. *Secret:* he hasn't told the major about the vented brands. *Voice:* earnest and formal West Point English, with a confessional streak.

**Abel Musgrave** (`musgrave`) — Whitepeak trading post (`store_whitepeak`) — mountain rancher.
*Motivation:* to be left alone with his snow. *Secret:* he sent Coyle's trackers east. *Voice:* laconic, in fragments of three to five words, and self-aware about his sales pitch.

**Tolani Ashe** (`tolani`) — Sayen Council House (`stable_sayen`) — horse breeder.
*Motivation:* a stock siding, coin sales and no railroad men north of the stones. *Secret:* she has already been corresponding with a Chicago buyer about the siding. *Voice:* direct, businesslike and proud. She has a dry wit about her brother and says hard truths without apology.

**Mareh Tiyo** (`mareh`) — Sayen lodgehouse — Council Speaker.
*Motivation:* agreements kept "in ink". *Secret:* she has personally re-set four treaty stones the surveyors "lost". *Voice:* patient, historical and quietly ironic. She speaks in long views and plain words.

**Isaac Redfern** (`isaac`) — Sayen lodgehouse — Council Secretary.
*Motivation:* win by law, and become the Territory's first Sayen attorney. *Secret:* he studies law by correspondence under an assumed initial. *Voice:* formal, legally precise and bitterly funny about letters nobody answers.

**Corin Ashe** (`corin`) — Sayen — wrangler, Tolani's brother.
*Motivation:* action over paper. *Secret:* he followed Kimbrough twice, meaning to shoot him, and couldn't. *Voice:* curt and restless, with grudging praise.

## Larkspur

**Sheriff Amos Whitlock** (`whitlock`) — sheriff's office. *Motivation:* get to his pension. *Secret:* his mortgage is held by the Ashby Commercial Bank, which is railroad-friendly. He's honest but afraid. *Voice:* weary, plain and self-aware: "I'm not bought. I'm just tired."

**Deputy Cyrus Dane** (`dane`). *Motivation:* adventure. *Secret:* he is a crack rifle shot and embarrassed by it. *Voice:* eager and digressive, a dime-novel romantic.

**Rufus Penhallow** (`rufus`) — Gilded Spur (`saloon_larkspur`). *Motivation:* a quiet bar. *Secret:* he waters nothing, and is proud of it. *Voice:* Cornish dryness, a gossip's economy.

**Gideon Platt** (`gideon`) — Gilded Spur regular, retired wrangler. *Motivation:* to see the Pale Mare once more. *Secret:* he let go of the rope in '81 on purpose. *Voice:* rambling and nostalgic, with tall-tale rhythm.

**Augustus Pettibone** (`pettibone`) — Pettibone Mercantile (`store_larkspur`). *Motivation:* solvency. *Secret:* he quietly forgives small debts and never admits it. *Voice:* fussy and pompous, comically scandalised by a gross of matches.

**Dr. Samuel Okafor** (`okafor`) — doctor's office (`doctor_larkspur`). *Motivation:* clean water and finished treatments. *Secret:* he has already written to Marshal Kincaid about the M&W bullet patch. *Voice:* precise, dry and Edinburgh-trained, deadpan.

**Hank Gatlin** (`gatlin`) — Gatlin Smithy (`smith_larkspur`). *Motivation:* honest work. *Secret:* none, which is rare. *Voice:* clipped fragments.

**Philippa Crane** (`crane`) — Calder House Hotel (`hotel_larkspur`). *Motivation:* propriety, and a good plot. *Secret:* she writes serials as "Colonel Rex Harland". *Voice:* starched and arch, with a wink buried in her etiquette.

**Rev. Elias Shaw** (`shaw`) — church. *Motivation:* clean water and short sermons. *Secret:* he buried 41 people at Gallow's Hope in 1879 and still keeps their register in his head. *Voice:* gentle and plain, with practical faith.

**Tilly Hargrove** (`tilly`) — child, telegraph operator's daughter. *Motivation:* Clementine. *Voice:* nine years old, run-on, earnest, with CAPITALS when excited.

## Port Ashby

**Deputy Marshal Eli Granger** (`granger`). Former Army scout. *Voice:* one to five words.
**Lionel Asquith** (`asquith`) — Lakeshore Hotel (`hotel_ashby`). *Secret:* he's from Cincinnati, not Surrey. *Voice:* fussy faux-English.
**Celeste Boudreau** (`celeste`) — The Lakeview (`saloon_ashby`). *Motivation:* keep her staff safe and paid. *Secret:* she keeps a list of what railroad men say when drunk. *Voice:* cool Creole-inflected understatement.
**Otto Ingram** (`ingram`) — Ingram Arms (`gun_ashby`). *Voice:* Bavarian, blunt, with imperative instructions.
**Hippolyte Delacroix** (`delacroix`) — Delacroix Clothier (`tailor_ashby`). *Voice:* theatrical French phrases, kind judgments of your cuffs.
**Chen Lian** (`lianchen`) — Water Street General Store (`store_ashby`). *Secret:* she extends credit to injured rail workers and keeps the company's insult letter framed. *Voice:* warm and wry.
**Dr. Harriet Lowell** (`lowell`) — doctor (`doctor_ashby`). *Voice:* brisk, and pre-empts condescension.
**Benito Ruiz** (`benito`) — Ashby Stables & Horse Exchange (`stable_ashby`), runs the Derby. *Secret:* he bought six falsely condemned Sayen mares. *Voice:* charming, with Spanish interjections and a conscience that shows.

## Brannigan Ranch

**Mateo Salinas** (`mateo`) — barn (`ranch_brannigan`). *Motivation:* the horses. *Secret:* he sings badly on purpose. *Voice:* cheerful, Spanish interjections, horse-care wisdom: "Standing still is the hardest trick there is."

## Sulfur Flats

**Conrad Vogel** (`vogel`) — assay office (`assay_sulfur`). *Motivation:* closure for Hester. *Voice:* German, precise, melancholy ("ja").
**Ike Mulroney** (`mulroney`) — The Dry Well (`saloon_sulfur`). *Voice:* cynical, an honest crook about his whiskey.
**Harlan Briggs** (`briggs`) — Company Store (`store_sulfur`). *Secret:* he hates canned peaches and his job. *Voice:* officious and apologetic.
**Pablo Ortega** (`ortega`) — stable (`stable_sulfur`). *Voice:* patient desert lore.
**Li Wen** (`liwen`) — contract miner, organising against scrip. *Motivation:* bring his brother from Guangdong. *Voice:* careful and wry. "Arithmetic is not organising. It is only true."

## Cedar Notch

**Marta Aune** (`marta`) — cookhouse (`store_cedar`). *Voice:* bossy Norwegian care, imperative sentences, "Ach".
**Rolf Haugen** (`rolf`) — injured faller. *Motivation:* money for his brother in Bergen. *Voice:* stoic and terse, "Ja".

## Whitepeak

**Ada Musgrave** (`ada`) — barn (`stable_whitepeak`). *Motivation:* win the Ashby Derby on Boulder. *Voice:* blunt and cheerful.

## Fort Rourke

**Sgt. Cornelius Fry** (`fry`) — barracks. A Tenth Cavalry veteran. *Voice:* parade-ground gruff, with quiet asides about lieutenants.
**Hiram Voss** (`voss`) — Sutler's Store (`store_fort`). *Secret:* he knows "Hadley" is Ned Tarrant. *Voice:* jovial huckster, everything is a commodity.

## Meridian Junction

**Walt Kessler** (`kessler`) — station (`station_meridian`). *Secret:* he keeps copies of interesting telegrams, against policy. *Voice:* lonely, chatty and self-deprecating.

## Halvorsen Homestead

**Ingrid Halvorsen** (`ingrid`). *Motivation:* never leave. *Voice:* Norwegian-inflected, stubborn and food-forward: "You will take bread."
**Nils Halvorsen** (`nils`). *Motivation:* keep his family alive through a dry year. *Voice:* anxious arithmetic.
**Morton Haskett** (`haskett`) — present only during `side_halvorsen` stage `confront`. Townsite "field representative". *Voice:* bluster that collapses under pressure.
